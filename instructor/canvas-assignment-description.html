Problem Set 2: What Is the Probability of Beating a Benchmark?

Release: Sunday, September 20, 2026
Initial submission deadline: Sunday, October 4, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

Use 2025 Apple (AAPL) prices to estimate the probability of beating a benchmark that grows at 5% per trading year, compounded continuously. Compare forecasts for 21, 63, and 126 trading days with what happened in 2026. Standard uses a binomial lattice. Advanced also uses geometric Brownian motion (GBM) and compares expected net present value (NPV) with the probability of beating the benchmark.
